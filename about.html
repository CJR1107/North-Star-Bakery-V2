Our story

A small bakery built around good recipes, early mornings, and neighbors who keep coming back.

01

Where it started

From home recipes to a neighborhood shop.

North Star Bakery started with a few bread recipes and many mornings spent getting them right. Our friends and family kept asking for more, so we decided to turn that hobby into a small neighborhood bakery.

We still like to keep things simple. We'd rather make a smaller batch fresh than fill the shelves with more than we need.

02

How we bake

Good ingredients, without making it complicated.

When possible, we buy ingredients from nearby suppliers and keep the menu focused on the basics. That gives us more time to pay attention to each batch.

Our breads, pastries, and cakes reflect what people around the neighborhood actually enjoy bringing home.
